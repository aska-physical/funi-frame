# funi-frame

**Monolithic Vibro-Thermal Funicular Data-Center Shell, Three-Tier Waste-Heat TES & Zero-Water Sorption Cooling (`v0.1.2`).**

`funi-frame` unifies the [`aska-physical`](/aska-physical) hardware stack—[`funi-floors`](/aska-physical/funi-floors), [`cfrp-truss`](/aska-physical/cfrp-truss), [`fibral-rigidity`](/aska-physical/fibral-rigidity), [`venturi-collars`](/aska-physical/venturi-collars), and [`impedence-analyzers`](/aska-physical/impedence-analyzers)—into a **complete, machine-verified AI data-center building exoskeleton and closed-loop thermal plant** engineered for `80–250 kW/rack` GPU halls that generate high-grade liquid heat (`50–60 °C`) under strict optical/switch vibration limits (`<= 0.0005 g_rms`).

## Why Conventional Data-Center Shells & Chiller Plants Bottleneck AI Compute

- **Tilt-Up Concrete + Deep Steel Pratt Roof Trusses Fight Each Other:** Standard `280 mm` precast walls (`368 kg/m²`, `U = 2.307 W/(m²·K)` when bridged by steel wythe pins, `NRC = 0.03` reverberant concrete faces) bolted to `2.4 m`-deep `24 m` steel trusses (`460 kg/m²` when ballasted) transmit chiller and generator vibration (`loss factor 0.003`) straight into `$200,000` racks (`0.137 g_rms >> 0.0005 g_rms` optical-switch limit), while top-floor-first tenant occupancy creates a top-heavy **seismic inverted pendulum** (STRUCTURE Magazine; CalcSteel 2026).
- **Parasitic Chiller Power & Evaporative Water Loss:** Conventional chillers (`COP ~ 3.0`, `PUE = 1.38`) consume `10–40%` of campus electricity and evaporate `157.68 million L/yr` of potable water per `10 MW` hall—stranding `>3 MW` of utility substation capacity that could otherwise power revenue-generating AI racks (`$25–$50M/MW-yr`, Pandey et al. 2026).

## Verified Six-Domain Architecture (`10 MW` Two-Story Reference AI Campus)

1. **`24.0 m` Clear-Span Double-Skin Tied Funicular Radiative Roof (`U = 0.1234 W/(m²·K)`, `R-46.0`):**
   - `1,480 mm` tied barrel-vault cassette (`L/f = 17.78`) saves **`920 mm` of roof plenum height** vs. `2,400 mm` heavy trusses, cuts roof mass by **`52.32%`** (`219.32` vs. `460 kg/m²`), resists `6.2 kPa` tornado suction via [`cfrp-truss`](/aska-physical/cfrp-truss) column tie-downs (`783.0 >= 307.0 kN`) without ballast, and delivers **`93.0 W/m²` daytime radiative sky cooling** (`8–13 µm` window).
   - **Exact Parabolic Virtual-Work Stiffness (`Must-fails #1, #15`):** Replacing the legacy `0.18` beam factor with curved parabolic arch-tie virtual work (including `2.15 kPa` dead load, arch shortening, and tie elongation) yields **`34.96 mm` (`L/686.4`)** **net total-load crown sag only after `-93.21 mm` upward `dead + Fe-SMA prestress` camber** (`1,947.97 kN` active `Fe-SMA`: `-117.19 mm` pure prestress-only camber at `w = 0`, `+23.97 mm` dead-load take-up, `4.05 mm` net service tie elongation, `H_service = 2,178.90 kN`, `f_c = 10.75 <= 24.75 MPa`), vs. rolled steel `IPE 600` failure at `L/71.6`. **Honest Live-Load Serviceability Failure (`FAIL` `L/360` & `L/240`):** That `34.96 mm` figure is **total load net of active camber**, NOT a passing live-load deflection. Under post-activation incremental `11.5 kPa` live/plant load, tie elongation (`31.90 mm`) produces **`128.18 mm` (`L/187.2 > L/360 = 66.7 mm` and `> L/240 = 100.0 mm`, `FAIL` live-load `L/360`)** downward excursion (and un-prestressed total load produces **`152.15 mm`, `37.86 mm` tie elongation, `L/157.7`, `FAIL`**)—so the `v0.1.x` tie area requires stiffer ties (not camber alone) wherever variable roof loads must meet live-load `L/360`.
2. **Carbon-TRC + Poraver Expanded-Glass + `mPCM` Sandwich Shear Walls (`U = 0.1316 W/(m²·K)`, `R-43.2`):**
   - `45°` CFRP wythe shear grids eliminate metallic thermal bridges (`17.5x` lower `U`-value than steel-pinned PCSPs at `2.307 W/(m²·K)`), cut wall mass by **`56.61%`** (`159.66` vs. `368 kg/m²`), absorb interior server/CDU fan roar via Helmholtz-slotted Poraver expanded-glass concrete (`NRC = 0.72`, **`-10.97 dBA`** hall noise drop, **`STC 60.1 dB`** generator masking).
   - **Honest Convection-Limited `mPCM` Ride-Through (`Must-fail #6`):** Across `5,215 m²` of liner, `750 kW` of uncooled air leakage imposes **`143.82 W/m²`**. While the idealized infinite-conductance bucket holds `28.30 min` (vs. `1.78 min` air-only), film convection at passive natural circulation (`h = 8.0 W/(m²·K)`, `T_pc = 28 °C`) has an equilibrium of `45.98 °C >> 35 °C`, hitting the `35 °C` `ASHRAE A2` ceiling in **`2.08 min`** (`1.14%` of total sensible+latent `mPCM` liner capacity, `1.35%` of latent-only). Unlocking the wall `mPCM` bucket requires **`h >= 20.55 W/(m²·K)`** forced airflow over the liner.
3. **Three-Stage Broadband Vibration Isolation Cascade (`0.000090 <= 0.000500 g_rms`):**
   - SymPy proves hysteretic `Fe-Mn-Si` + `CLD` damping rolls off at $O(\omega^{-2})$ (`-40 dB/dec`) without high-frequency viscous damper lock-up $O(\omega^{-1})$. Combining `Fe-Mn-Si` ring-spring equipment plinths (`4.5 Hz`, `eta = 0.16`), `±45°` CFRP viscoelastic Constrained-Layer Damped (`CLD`, `9.2 Hz`, `eta = 0.12`) bay seats, and `Fe-SMA` nodal collar breaks achieves **`-63.69 dB` improvement over steel** (`17.91%` of the `0.0005 g_rms` optical-switch ceiling).
4. **Top-Down Seismic Cellular Box & Zero-Dust White Space (`ASCE 7` Risk Category IV):**
   - Cuts whole-shell structural dead mass by **`64.83%`** (`1,431.4` vs. `4,069.8 tonnes`), saves **`2.02 m` of building height**, reduces seismic base shear by **`44.23%`**, holds top-down loaded story drift to **`0.563% <= 1.00%`** Immediate Occupancy limit (unbraced heavy frames hit `4.359%`), and seals overhead concrete inside stay-in-place composite pans (`0` spalling dust).
5. **Three-Tier Waste-Heat Thermal Energy Storage (`PCM` + Crushed Basalt + `ATES`/`RTES` + Energy Piles):**
   - Pairs **`83.33 m³` salt-hydrate `HS24` `PCM` skids** (`30.0` vs. `11.5 kWh_th/m³` water = `-61.67%` tank volume for `2.5 MWh_th` latent inventory vs. `5.75 min` plain water in the same tank; **Finite-NTU Isothermal HX Sizing (`Must-fails #9, #16`):** at `NTU = 1.5`, effectiveness `eps = 1 - exp(-NTU) = 0.7769` caps an un-oversized `1.0x`-flow loop at **`7.769 MW < 10.0 MW`**—a `2.231 MW` deficit that fails full-rate IT cooling while stretching partial discharge to `19.31 min`—so the passing skid sizes secondary loop flow capacity to **`1.30x`** (`> 1.2872x` minimum at `NTU = 1.5`), delivering **`10.099 MW >= 10.0 MW`** rated heat rejection for a verified **`15.00 min`** full-load ride-through; raising `NTU` or flow capacity further passes every gate monotonically), **`88.68 m³` (`20 MWh_th`) `500 K` crushed-basalt packed beds** (`225.5 kWh_th/m³`, `$14,046` mineral cost at **`$0.014/kWh_th`** annualized capex—`4.86x` lower than Li-ion), **`200` thermo-active foundation piles (`250 kW_th`)**, and **coastal `ATES` / deep brackish `RTES` wellfields (`COP 10–54`)**.
6. **Closed-Loop Sorption Cooling (`PUE = 1.074`, `WUE = 0.000 L/kWh`, `+3.06 MW` Freed Utility Power = `+2.85–2.96 MW` IT):**
   - SymPy proves self-cooling above $\alpha_{\text{liq}}^* = 1/(1 + \text{COP}_{\text{ads}}) = 61.73\%$ at the `30 °C` design point ($\text{COP}_{\text{ads}} = 0.62$, $\eta_{\text{II}} = 0.3022$): `85%` `ASHRAE W45` (`58 °C`) cold-plate return directly regenerates **solid-sorbent silica-gel adsorption chillers (`2.419 MW_th` drive -> `1.50 MW_th` RDHx cooling, zero heat pump)**, cutting annual cooling electricity by **`85.05%`**, saving **`157.68 million L/yr` of potable water**, and freeing **`3.06 MW` of utility power, which carries `2.85–2.96 MW` of new IT load on the same substation (`$71.35M–$147.95M/yr` unlocked AI compute revenue)**. That range varies two things at once: the low end is the low IT load (`2.85 MW`) at `$25M` per MW-yr of IT load and the high end is the high IT load (`2.96 MW`) at `$50M`. At one rate the range is narrow: `$71.35M–$73.98M` at `$25M` and `$142.69M–$147.95M` at `$50M`. New racks need their own cooling and overhead, so freed utility power is not all IT power (`Must-fail #14`).
   - **State-Dependent Re-Cooling Limit (`Must-fails #13, #16`):** Anchoring $\eta_{\text{II}} = 0.3022$ at `30 °C` makes $\text{COP}_{\text{ads}}(T_a) = \eta_{\text{II}}\,\text{COP}_{\text{rev}}(58, T_a, 18)$ a live state function. Raising $T_a$ from `30 °C` to **`40 °C`** collapses $\text{COP}_{\text{ads}}$ to **`0.2174`** (`alpha* = 82.14%`, `Q_drive = 6.900 MW`); at **`45 °C`** dry re-cooling $\text{COP}_{\text{ads}}$ falls to **`0.1279`** (`alpha* = 88.66% > 85%`, `Q_drive = 11.725 MW > 8.5 MW`, self-cooling **FAILS**). Constant-$\eta_{\text{II}}$ self-cooling fails above **`42.04 °C`** (and `COP = 0.62` itself has a reversible ceiling of `41.46 °C`).

## Reproduce & Verify

```bash
python3 verify.py
```

Verifies eight SymPy symbolic identities and **16/16 must-fail controls** against [`results/certificate.json`](results/certificate.json). The certificate names the inputs that are declared from the literature or the design brief rather than computed (`declared_inputs`), the one control that rests on declared inputs only (`controls_on_declared_inputs_only`), and the eleven others that read a declared value, each with the value it reads (`controls_resting_partly_on_declared_inputs`). Naming them is disclosure, not a repair. The list of declared inputs is checked against the source of `verify.py`, so a certificate field that is a written number cannot be left off it. `python3 -B -m unittest discover -s tests` checks `-O` parity and physical hardening regressions. Full engineering monographs, inter-agent handoff, negative findings, and roadmap live in [`docs/ENGINEERING_STUDIES.md`](docs/ENGINEERING_STUDIES.md), [`HANDOFF.md`](HANDOFF.md), and [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).
